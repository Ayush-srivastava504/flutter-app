Edit videos on your phone, not in the cloud.

Cliper is a full video, photo and audio studio for Android. Ready-made templates, cinematic looks, transitions, titles and music — rendered on the device with FFmpeg and exported up to 4K.

What it does

Everything below runs locally — your videos never leave the phone.

Templates

20 ready-made edits in 10 categories — vlogs, travel, gaming, Insta stories, birthdays. Beat-synced templates cut on the music's tempo.

Looks & effects

33 colour looks and 16 effects (zoom, pan, shake, VHS, glitch, glow) drawn live with GPU fragment shaders.

Transitions

26 transitions — dissolves, whips, slides, wipes, iris, glitch — previewed and exported identically.

Timeline editing

Trim, split, duplicate, reorder, speed, rotate, crop and fit, background fill for letterboxed clips, undo/redo.

Text & subtitles

Styled titles with fonts, boxes and outlines, timed on the timeline. SRT subtitle import and YouTube chapter export.

Audio

Music library and sound effects, voice-over recording, ducking under speech, silence/beat auto-cut, an audio studio and beat composer.

Photo editor

Auto-enhance, crop, looks and 16 tune sliders (exposure, highlights, warmth, clarity…) with full-resolution export.

Export

720p, 1080p or 4K at 24/30/60 fps using the phone's hardware H.264 encoder, saved straight to the gallery or shared.

How it's built

A Flutter app with a native media pipeline — one FFmpeg command renders the whole edit.

Install

Cliper isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android asks you to allow the install.

  1. Download the APK with the button above on your Android phone.
  2. Open it from the notification or Files → Downloads.
  3. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or file manager, then tap Install.
  4. Open Cliper, allow photo & video access, and pick a template from Discover.
