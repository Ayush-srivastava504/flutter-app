ClipForge audio library (assets/audio/library/*.m4a and assets/audio/sfx/*.m4a)

Every track and sound effect in these two folders was synthesised from scratch by
tools/make_audio_library.py (oscillators, plucked-string physics, noise drums and
reverb written in that script). No samples, loops, presets or third-party recordings
were used, so there is no outside copyright or licence attached.

The project owner dedicates these files to the public domain under CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): anyone may use them for any
purpose, including monetised YouTube / Instagram videos, without attribution.
None of them is registered with YouTube Content ID or any similar system.

(The three older loops in assets/audio/*.wav are NOT covered by this note — see
docs/FACTS.md F-81.)
