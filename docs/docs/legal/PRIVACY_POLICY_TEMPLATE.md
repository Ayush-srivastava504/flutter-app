## 1. Summary
ClipForge is a video editor that works **on your device**. It does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not send your videos, photos, audio, or project data to us or to any third party.

## 2. Information we do not collect
We do not collect, store on a server, sell, or share personal information. The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting service. [Remove or edit this sentence if that changes.]

## 3. What stays on your device
- **Your media** (videos, photos, music) is accessed through the Android system picker when you choose it. Your original files are never modified.
- **Your projects** (edit instructions such as trim points, filters, text, and music settings) are stored in a database inside the app's private storage on your device.
- **Temporary files** (for example copies of imported media, thumbnails, and exported videos before you save them) are stored in the app's private storage and may be deleted by the system or when you uninstall the app.
- **Exported videos** are saved to your device's gallery (album "ClipForge") when you export. They are yours; we do not receive a copy.
